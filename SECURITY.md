# Privacy Policy for Game-Pointer

Last updated: September 21, 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how **Game-Pointer** handles your information and data.

## 1. Information Collection and Usage

**Game-Pointer** is designed with privacy in mind:

- **No Personal Data Collection:** We do not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, IP address, or location.
- **No Tracking or Analytics:** The project does not integrate third-party tracking, analytics, or telemetry services to monitor your usage or behavior.
- **Local Operations:** Any configuration files, user preferences, or application states are stored locally on your device and are never transmitted to external servers.

## 2. Third-Party Services and External Links

If **Game-Pointer** interacts with any third-party APIs or external websites, please note that those services have their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review the privacy statements of any third-party services you connect with.

## 3. Data Security

Since **Game-Pointer** does not collect or transmit personal data over the internet, your information remains entirely on your local machine and under your control.

## 4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted directly to this repository. You are advised to review this page periodically for any updates.

## 5. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or suggestions regarding this Privacy Policy, feel free to open an issue in the repository:
[github.com/gotar13/Game-Pointer/issues](https://github.com/gotar13/Game-Pointer/issues)
